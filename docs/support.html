How it works

  1. Tap Continue to payment. You'll go to Verify Checkout, which shows RaGoose's account and how to pay.

  2. Send the amount you choose with telebirr, CBE Birr or Dashen Bank, then enter the transaction reference you get.

  3. Verify Checkout confirms the transfer and brings you back here.

Your money goes straight to RaGoose's account. Verify Checkout only confirms the transfer. It never holds your money, and gramX never sees your payment.
